## Captured text

F.O.S is an established clothing retail chain that specializes in affordable quality apparel. The inaugural opening of its outlet in Sunway Pyramid in 1997, during the infamous economic crisis in Malaysia, marked the birth of F.O.S. From that single outlet in Sunway Pyramid, F.O.S has grown to a total of more than 90 outlets to date throughout major shopping complexes located in the Peninsular as well as Sabah & Sarawak. The number of outlets is constantly growing with the opening of each new major and exciting shopping complex throughout the country.
