## Captured text

In corporated in 1996, Chrisna Jenio Sdn Bhd has embarked as one of the pioneer of modern snack operator in Malaysia. Being the master franchise holder that operates Auntie Anne's concept in Malaysia, Chrisna Jenio is the first to introduce and offer, fresh, hand rolled soft pretzels. With an open kitchen concept and products being made in a full view of customers, Auntie Anne’s had since serve millions of pretzels to Malaysian pretzel lovers. Having 67 locations* across Peninsular Malaysia (except KLIA 2) with around 250 employees, we are serving 9 millions freshly baked yearly. *as of Sep 2024.
